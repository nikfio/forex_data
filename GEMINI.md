## UTC Datetime Constraint & Timezone Policy (MANDATORY)

1. **Universal Naive UTC Standard**:
   - All internal timestamps, database parquet files, event tables, observation timestamps, and cached records across `forex-data` must be stored and handled as **timezone-naive UTC** (`datetime` or `pd.Timestamp` with `tzinfo=None`).
2. **External Interface Normalization**:
   - Any public or interface function accepting external dates (strings, ISO formats, integer/float timestamps, timezone-aware datetimes) must immediately normalize them to timezone-naive UTC via `normalize_to_naive_utc()` before performing arithmetic, comparisons, business day counts, or database queries.
3. **No Mixing Offset-Aware & Offset-Naive**:
   - Never compare (`<`, `<=`, `>`, `>=`) or subtract offset-aware and offset-naive datetimes directly to avoid Python `TypeError: can't compare offset-naive and offset-aware datetimes`.
